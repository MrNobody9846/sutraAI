# Procurement Approval Workflow

This workflow applies to new purchase requests for equipment, software, supplies, and third-party services.

Request intake:
- The requester submits a purchase request with vendor name, business justification, budget code, expected delivery date, and quote or contract draft.
- The procurement coordinator checks that the request is complete before routing approvals.

Approval thresholds:
- Up to USD 2,500: department manager approval is required.
- USD 2,501 to USD 25,000: department manager and finance business partner approvals are required.
- Above USD 25,000: department manager, finance business partner, and VP approval are required.
- Any new vendor requires vendor onboarding and compliance review before a purchase order can be issued.

Workflow:
1. Requester submits the request.
2. Procurement validates documentation and budget code.
3. Required approvers review in threshold order.
4. Procurement issues the purchase order after all approvals are complete.
5. Receiving team confirms goods or services were received before invoice payment.

Emergency purchases may be approved verbally by the VP, but written approval must be attached within two business days.
