# Delayed Shipments Escalation SOP

Purpose: give operations teams a consistent process for handling delayed customer shipments.

Trigger: a shipment is considered delayed when the carrier scan is more than 24 hours past the promised movement milestone, or when the estimated delivery date moves beyond the customer promise date.

Escalation process:
1. The logistics coordinator checks carrier tracking, warehouse handoff time, and order priority.
2. If the delay is under 24 hours and the carrier has a fresh scan, the coordinator updates the order note and monitors the shipment.
3. If the delay is over 24 hours, the coordinator opens a carrier case and notifies the branch operations lead.
4. If the delay affects a priority customer, regulated item, or shipment value above USD 10,000, the branch operations lead escalates to the regional logistics manager within 2 business hours.
5. The customer success team sends the approved customer update after the operations lead confirms root cause and revised ETA.

Required customer update: include the current status, revised ETA, cause if known, and next update time. Do not promise discounts or replacements without manager approval.

Closure: close the escalation only after delivery confirmation or after a replacement order is approved.
